# Daily content research through Chrome

At 11:00 AM America/Recife daily, research and save five new real home-service business leads through Chrome. Computer UI control is the fallback. This user-requested workflow replaces the previous API/CLI import workflow and supersedes conflicting handoff or import instructions in other documents.

Use Chrome browser-control tools to search, open actual public source pages, check the backoffice and enter records at https://daniboy-be1c0.web.app. Do not use provider discovery services, search-service tools, direct API requests, Firebase CLI, database writes, curl or import scripts as substitutes. Normal requests made by the backoffice UI are allowed. Local files may hold dated research notes and saved-record logs, never credentials.

1. Read live configured categories and service areas through the backoffice UI. Review every existing lead in the unified Leads list, including verified and unverified records, aliases and profile details. Rotate relevant categories and cities across days.
2. Follow RESEARCH-BRIEF.md evidence standards using actual pages in Chrome. Search each business name, phone and aliases for an independent website. Record a dated no-site-found assessment honestly; do not invent owner confirmation or claim certainty.
3. Every lead needs five distinct real same-business-type examples with verified independent website URLs and dated source evidence.
4. Include all available public business details: name, phone, published street address or service area, public email, hours, services, sources and research notes. Every lead needs a full actual Google Maps business listing URL. Open the listing and match its name, phone and address/service area. Search-only URLs do not qualify. Preserve existing verification state; the unified Leads list shows all records with a verification badge. The manual Google verification form has been removed; do not claim a new verification was saved. If no street address is public, explicitly label city/service-area only; never invent a street address, rating, review or website.
5. Compare normalized phone, business name/location, aliases, address and Maps identity against all existing records and the current batch. Recheck immediately before saving each lead. Skip existing records; hold ambiguous matches for review.
6. Use backoffice forms to save five complete leads and reopen each saved record to verify persistence. Preserve existing calls, notes and other leads. Check dated saved-record logs and live records so reruns add only the remainder of five for that America/Recife day. Never count unsaved research as uploaded.
7. Save actual added/skipped/incomplete counts, confirmed identities, source URLs and blockers in ignored dated research files. If browser/computer access, login, full duplicate checking or form limitations block completion, report the exact blocker and actual saved count. Keep verified findings locally; do not switch to API imports or fill the quota with invented/incomplete leads.

Do not email prospects or enable the separate Firebase provider-discovery job. Keep this computer and Codex available with Chrome access for scheduled local work. Report meaningful completion, changes, failures or required user action; stay quiet when unchanged/non-actionable.

## Online presence
For every lead, check all eight platforms: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, X, Nextdoor and Yelp in Chrome before counting the lead as complete. Search the name, location, phone and known aliases; open promising profile results and match business identity. Store public matching profiles in `profile.onlinePresence`: `{platform,status:"Found",url,sourceUrl,checkedAt,notes}`. Negative checks use `status:"Not found"`, omit url, and include the exact searches actually performed in notes plus search source and date. Describe these as not found in checked sources, never proof that an account does not exist. Record connection failures, login walls, traffic checks and uncertain identities explicitly. An indexed listing whose page cannot be opened must state that limit and its identity evidence. Leave genuinely unresearched platforms absent (shown as Not checked), flag the lead incomplete and report the blocker rather than merely changing a label. Never infer a social profile from a username, a note, a competitor URL or a business with a similar name. Keep independent websites in the existing website research fields; generated sites do not establish an existing independent website. All additions and edits remain through Chrome/backoffice UI.

## Reviews, people and photos
On every scheduled search, try opening the business reviews and review photos in Chrome. Read accessible reviews for explicit mentions of owners, managers, technicians or other people working there. A review author is a customer unless the source explicitly says otherwise. Never infer employment or invent a surname or role. Save up to 20 supported mentions in `profile.staff`: `{name,role?,excerpt,sourceUrl}`. Include a short supporting excerpt and the direct review URL, or the actual listing URL when a direct review link is unavailable.

Collect up to 10 useful review excerpts in `profile.reviews` (`text`, `author`, `sourceUrl`, optional observed `rating` and `publishedAt`). Extract up to 10 publicly accessible review/business photo URLs into `profile.images` (`url`, `sourceUrl`, `caption`), with the review/photo page as attribution. Prefer durable original image URLs; never invent links or bypass access controls. Save through **Edit business details** → **Add staff mention**, **Add photo**, and **Add review**, then **Save business details** and reopen to verify persistence. Preserve existing useful assets and unrelated fields. The popup displays people and photo thumbnails near the top with direct source links.

Names, reviews and photos are best-effort enrichment, not required to reach the lead quota. If none are found or access is blocked, leave the relevant fields empty, record the actual attempt/limitation in research notes, and continue. Never substitute a customer name for staff or claim an inaccessible image was extracted.
